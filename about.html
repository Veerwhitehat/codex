Mumbai Maker's Collective

Karigar Connect is a social-commerce platform that places artisans at the center of digital retail. We connect Mumbai's informal craft clusters to national buyers through story-rich pages, trusted sourcing details, and simple mobile-first interactions.

Our mission is to transform marketplaces into equitable ecosystems: each sale supports dignified income, preserves generational craft, and strengthens local supply chains.
